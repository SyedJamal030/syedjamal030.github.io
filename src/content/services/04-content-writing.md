Great design and code only succeed when paired with clear, persuasive communication. If visitors cannot instantly figure out what your product does, why it matters, and how to get started, they leave.

I write clear website copy and ghostwrite structured articles for companies, founders, and technical teams who need to articulate their value without relying on marketing buzzwords or corporate jargon.

## Writing Focus Areas

- **Website Copywriting:** Landing page copy, product features, value propositions, service descriptions, and call-to-action messaging structured around human reading patterns.
- **Article & Founder Ghostwriting:** Researching, outlining, and writing long-form articles, opinion pieces, and educational blog posts published directly under your name or brand.
- **SEO & Intent Alignment:** Structuring content with clear headings, natural keyphrase integration, and concise answer blocks designed to rank well on search engines and answer user queries directly.
- **Brand Voice Adaptation:** Adapting writing style to match your brand persona, whether that calls for a grounded technical tone, an energetic startup pitch, or an authoritative industry analysis.

## Content Delivered Ready for Publishing

All written deliverables are provided in clean Markdown or HTML formats, formatted with semantic headings, bullet lists, and meta description suggestions. This allows your team or web managers to paste content straight into your CMS or repository without spent hours fixing styling or structural formatting.
